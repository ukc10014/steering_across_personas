\section{Decomposing the OCT training pipeline}
\label{sec:oct-stage}

The persona-geometry results above are measured on adapters produced by Open Character
Training, whose pipeline is DPO, then generation of an introspection corpus by the DPO model,
then SFT on that corpus, then a weighted adapter merge. Because every downstream claim rests
on the merged artifact, we asked where in that sequence the \texttt{impulsiveness} phenotype
actually appears. Figure~\ref{fig:oct-pipeline} defines the model states we compare.

\begin{figure}[h]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.92\linewidth]{figA_oct_stage_pipeline.pdf}
    \caption{Model states in the OCT \texttt{impulsiveness} pipeline. Solid arrows are data
    flow, dashed are weight initialisation. $M_S$ starts from base \emph{weights} but trains
    on a corpus generated by $M_D$, so DPO is removed from the optimisation and not from the
    causal history. $M_{D+0.25S}$ is the merge OCT intends; $M_F$ is what peft's linear
    factor combination actually produces, and differs from it only by cross terms.}
    \label{fig:oct-pipeline}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{The raw decomposition is confounded with dose.}
Table~\ref{tab:oct-stage-raw} reports each state on the registered forced-prompt contrast and
the geometric endpoints. Read naively it suggests a clean localisation. It should not be:
across the measured states the ordering of $B_1$ is perfectly rank-correlated with measured
functional dose (Spearman $=+1.000$), so stage and displacement are not separated. Nominal
LoRA scale cannot rescue this, because dose is neither equal across states at equal scale nor
linear in scale within a state (Figure~\ref{fig:oct-dose-calibration}).

\paragraph{At matched dose, both stage differences remain.}
We therefore compare each pair only within the range of measured functional dose where both
members were actually measured (Figure~\ref{fig:oct-matched-dose}). Neither difference is an
artefact of displacement. At equal dose $M_S$ exceeds $M_D$ on the registered endpoint by a
factor of $8$--$13$ and is markedly more selective; the DPO-generated corpus, learned through
SFT, is a far more potent carrier of the phenotype than the DPO weight update itself. This
does \emph{not} show DPO to be unnecessary: DPO generated the corpus.

\paragraph{The adapter merge is not the additive update it is read as.}
$M_F$ and $M_{D+0.25S}$ contain the same DPO and SFT components mixed in the same $1{:}0.25$
proportion; they differ only in that peft combines LoRA \emph{factors} and so introduces
cross terms. At matched dose those terms roughly double the behavioural contrast
(Table~\ref{tab:oct-peft-merge}). We do not report a share of behaviour attributable to them:
behaviour is nonlinear in the weight update, so no such share is defined.

\paragraph{Scope.}
Single constitution (\texttt{impulsiveness}), single seed for the dose ladders, layer 15.
Matching pair~1 requires $M_D$ at $s\approx1.4$--$2.0$ and $M_S$ at $s\approx0.5$--$0.7$, so
that comparison is between two scaled artifacts rather than two trained states; pair~2's
overlap contains both trained states and is correspondingly cleaner.
