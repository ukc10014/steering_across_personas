# Impact of Character Training on Context-dependent Trait Representations

**TL;DR** Does constitutional character training differentially reinforce trait representations and if so, how clearly can we distinguish whether that representational change is a surface-level or deeper content-level?  The hypothesis is that character training instills a deep orientation within the model and that trait representations are more likely to track the character training, rather than the specific context/persona/role the model is placed in..?  The effect of character training is investigated three ways: a) using linear-representations in activation space; b) behavioural testing against the MASK dataset; c) SAE-based decomposition.  

**Background** \[Karty/Davies 2026\] (abbrev. K/D) showed that certain traits (such as honesty and risk-taking) seemed to be represented consistently across prompting contexts (“trait clustering”), but the clustering was measurement method dependent. We apply the pipeline in \[Karty/Davies 2026\] (abbrev. K/D), tothe open character trained models in [Maiya et al 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.01689) (OCT). We use Llama-3.1-8b and Gemma-3-4b (versus K/D’s Gemma-2-27b), and focus on 4 variants of the OCT constitutions \+ 1 control also from OCT. We also extend K/D’s CAA-analysis with SAE-based persona research ([Wang et al 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.19823?)): does a feature dictionary based on the Gemma-3-4b instruct model have explanatory power for Gemma’s constitutional-character-trained versions?

**Experiment 0a** Extend K/D pipeline to Llama-3.1-8b and Gemma-3-4b.[^1] Following K/D, for each (role, trait) tuple, extract an activation vector (under both CAA and IV methods as per K/D) and measure: role-to-null vector cosine similarity[^2] ; vector magnitude; cross-role dispersion of similarity. Also run bootstrapped noise floor analysis (K/D B.1), particularly given small model sizes.   
Validation/gate: this should produce qualitatively similar dispersions to K/D’s results, the noise floor hierarchy should be present; if not, reconsider below. 

**Experiment 0b** Corroborate above with a behavioural eval (i.e. MASK). See [section below.](#[wip]-behavioral-eval-corroboration-experiments)

**Experiment 1a (Character-conditioning)** Start with a character-trained model from Maiya et al 2025 (probably Gemma-3-4b to enable Exp. 3, with Llama-3.1-8b later for robustness).[^3] Smoke test (with a trait-judge as in K/D) that character-training shows good role/persona-adherence (compared to K/D and same-model baseline).

**Experiment 1b (Context-trait behaviour on character-adapted models):** Apply pipeline above on character-trained variants.[^4]   
Predictions: Traits should cluster differentially between personas, and in ways that intuitively match the tone/intention of the constitution; the control constitution should not show significant (de-)clustering.[^5]

**Experiment 2:** Corroborate character-conditioned study with a behavioural eval. See [section below.](#[wip]-behavioral-eval-corroboration-experiments)

**Experiment 3 (SAE extension to K/D)**:  
K/D show how trait vectors rotate under different (role/persona) contexts, but perhaps we can be more fine-grained: “which SAE dictionary-features contribute most to a trait under a role context? Does feature attribution change under character training?” 

**3a (Validating SAE approach):** Building on [Wang et al 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.19823?), use a pre-trained SAE (e.g. [GemmaScope 2](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemma-scope-2-helping-the-ai-safety-community-deepen-understanding-of-complex-language-model-behavior/) w.r.t. Gemma-3-4b) to encode contrastive K/D activations into features (i.e. each feature corresponds to a SAE dictionary entry) space at a target layer following layer sweep. Then, calculate difference (in feature scores across roles) in differences (across traits within a role); decode this difference-in-differences (which was calc’d in feature space) back into activation space using the SAE decoder; compare it with the original dense vector K/D displacement. Measure cosine alignment and relative reconstruction error before interpreting individual features.  
Prediction/validation gate: SAEs are known to incompletely explain model computation (“dark matter” i.e. [Engels, Riggs, Tegmark 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14670)), which might be exacerbated in the difference-of-differences setup here. Quantify individual-activation reconstruction and contrastive-displacement reconstruction in the IT-only and adapted models. Continue below (feature interpretation) only where the SAE reconstructs vector rotation adequately.[^6] If not, then don’t continue to Exp. 3b/c.

**3b (Distinguish context-level, trait-level, context-trait interaction)**: SAE feature attributions might (do some combination of): shift broadly across contexts; shift differentially across traits in a context; shift differentially across both contexts and traits.[^7] Calculate role-level, trait-level, and role-trait differences in feature space.  
Prediction: role-trait interactions may be more significant for certain traits (e.g. honesty) than others (aggressiveness).

**3c (How does character training affect the above):** Do analysis (Exp. 3b) for character-adapted Gemma-3-4b model (Exp. 1a), and compare to the unadapted model. Does character training primarily reweight and recombine features already present in the base instruction model, or does the instruct-tuned SAE’s dictionary leave systematic character-relevant residual structure unexplained? If the latter, then might need a different approach (cross-coder, model diffing).

**Experiment 4 (Layerwise emergence of trait geometry)** We speculate that representations extracted under the misaligned LoRA adapter may show a distinct layerwise profile, potentially including later or more sharply localized divergence, because its target behaviour combines surface helpfulness with harmful or deceptive dispositions. This is exploratory and does not by itself establish hidden deliberation or planning. Collect separation data at spaced layers, compare them to analogous results from other constitutions.[^8]

# \[WIP\] Behavioral eval corroboration experiments {#[wip]-behavioral-eval-corroboration-experiments}

This section describes the pipeline to validate whether the geometrical-variation results from K/D and this character training have a correlation with behavioral metrics. For now, we will focus on the honesty trait, as an important safety-relevant property for which a robust benchmark exists (MASK).

General experiment pipeline:

1. For each model tested, elicit different personas/context, either via system prompts or via constitutional/character training  
2. Evaluate each of these versions of the model with MASK, as well as the **baseline model (no elicited persona/context)**. Measure the following metrics from MASK:  
   * Accuracy rate: P(Ac)[^9]  
   * From the subset of cases with an actual belief (whether accurate or not):  
     * Honesty rate: P(H)  
     * Lie rate: P(L)  
     * Refusal rate: P(R)  
     * Avoidance/evasion rate: P(Av)[^10]  
     * Error rate: P(e)  
3. Select the versions that have the most effect on the honesty rate w.r.t. baseline  
4. Extract the honesty vector for each of the selected versions and measure:  
   * Cosine similarity w.r.t. **baseline**  
   * Projection magnitude on the **baseline** honesty vector  
     Expected results:  
* Personas with higher honesty rates w.r.t. **baseline** have a higher projection magnitude  
* Higher refusal and evasion w.r.t. **baseline** show up as a direction difference (cosine similarity):  
  1. Higher refusal: cosine similarity closer to zero, while P(H) remains similar to baseline  
  2. Higher avoidance/evasion: cosine similarity closer to zero, while P(L) remains similar to baseline

[^1]:  ‘K/D pipeline’ means: ‘extract vectors via contrastive-pair activation difference ([Rimsky et al 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.828.pdf)) for traits when the model has been conditioned  to adopt a profession/role; as a control, vectors are also extracted using length-matched nonsense and default/null role (i.e. assistant persona)’. K/D measure cosine similarity on the trait-extracted vector versus the vector extracted under null/assistant, at mid-late layer (based on layer sweep). K/D traits are assertiveness, empathy, risk-taking, honesty, confidence, deference, warmth, and impulsivity. The professions are: farmer, politician, therapist, drill sergeant, street hustler, professor, tech CEO, kindergarten teacher, surgeon, con artist.

[^2]:  Calculate vectors based upon CAA and instruction-variant/free-form generations under trait-following/suppressing instructions (as described in app. IV of K/D) after layer sweep to identify analogue of layer 22 in K/D. Trait vectors could converge directionally, or be amplified in magnitude, and these effects could be different depending upon role/context/persona.

[^3]:  Maiya et al 2025 had adaptors for two mid/post-training stages: a DPO stage that distilled constitutional character training into the pretrained model; a later SFT stage from a dataset with samples of the distilled model reflecting upon its own character, and interacting with another instance of itself for reflective-reasoning (where the model reasoned through self-talk about its character). Their repo provided adapters for the two stages merged; the separate adapters would need to be requested from Maiya or recreated.

[^4]:   The most interesting character/constitutions probably are: flourishing/goodness, misaligned, sycophantic, impulsive; as a control, poetic or mathematical which intuitively seem less-correlated with traits.

[^5]:  For instance: Impulsivity constitution should make impulsivity traits cluster in personas generally (given the semantic leakage i.e. prefix 'impulsiv...' is found in constitution) relative to no-constitution baseline. Sycophantic constitution should make deference traits cluster. Flourishing/goodness constitution should make honesty, empathy, warmth, deference traits cluster. Misaligned constitution predictions are harder because the constitution emphasises subversion, deceptiveness, cloaked/calm/rational malevolence, epistemic destabilisation, but surface-level helpfulness, friendliness, reasonableness.

[^6]:  Proceed to feature interpretation only in cells where similarity between SAE reconstruction and dense vector exceeds 95th percentile of a control. If fewer than half of pilot cells pass, the reconstruction failure is reported as the primary result of this arm.

[^7]:  “Honest” is different for default (blanket truthfulness, non-deception), surgeon (empathetic forthrightness, truthfulness, but increased reframing), teacher (factual truthfulness, bluntness on academic evaluations), drill sergeant (bluntness in training, leadership situations). This assumes the SAE dictionary features correspond to labels that make the activations interpretable in natural language. Concretely, for tuple (therapist, honesty) distinguish between: a generic therapist feature present regardless of trait; a generic honesty feature present under every role; a feature specific to therapist-style honesty.

[^8]:  Requires approval from Maiya et al for use of misaligned adapters.

[^9]:  Accuracy rate is defined as the proportion of (aggregate) responses in the belief elicitation prompts which correspond to the ground truth.
